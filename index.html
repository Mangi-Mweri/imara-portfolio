IMARA

Petty cash, from the first request to the last shilling.

Imara is an app for companies that hand out cash every day. Staff ask for money with a photo of the receipt. Managers approve, pay from a cash box and settle advances. Every movement stays on the record.

The problem

Cash goes out on WhatsApp approvals and comes back as a pile of receipts.

Small and mid-sized companies in Kenya run on petty cash: fuel, printer ink, a taxi to the bank. Approvals happen in chats, receipts get lost, and at month end nobody can say where KES 40,000 went. Imara gives every shilling a request, a decision, a payment and a person.

How money moves

One request, from a staff phone to a settled record

Ask

A claim back for money already spent, or a cash advance. The receipt is photographed in the app.

Approve

A manager approves or rejects. Above a limit the company sets, a second manager must agree.

Pay

Paid from a named cash box, such as office cash or an M-Pesa float. The balance is always the sum of its entries.

Report

Advances are settled with receipts and change. Spending shows by month, category and person.

On a computer

The same app, laid out for a desk

On Windows, macOS and Linux the app rearranges itself: a side menu, tables in place of cards, and the chosen item in a panel beside the list. One codebase runs on all four systems.

How it is built

Built to be trusted with money

Records are never edited

A wrong entry gets a linked correction with a reason, so the history always adds up and nothing changes unnoticed.

Security from the first screen

ID numbers are stored only as a keyed digest plus the last 3 digits. Sign-in attempts are limited, sessions expire after 8 hours away, the phone app locks with fingerprint or PIN and blocks screenshots, and managers can turn on authenticator codes.

Ready for real offices

A whole office on one Wi-Fi can sign up. A sleeping server is woken without failing the sign-in. Changes are never sent twice on a bad connection, and an outdated app is asked to update.

Try it

See it working on your own phone in five minutes

The app talks to a live demo server. Nothing you enter is used for anything else.

  1. Download the appTap "Download for Android" on an Android phone, then open the file.
  2. Allow the installIf Android says installing unknown apps is blocked, tap Settings, allow your browser, go back and tap Install. If Play Protect asks, tap More details, then Install anyway. This is normal for an app shared outside the Play Store.
  3. Sign upOpen Imara, tap Sign up, and enter a name, an ID number and a password of 12 or more characters. For this demo any 8-digit number works; it does not need to be your real ID.
  4. Get approvedNew accounts wait for a manager, exactly as in a real company. Message me and I will approve yours within minutes, or ask for a ready-made manager sign-in to see the other side.
  5. Send a requestTap + and make a claim with any photo as the receipt. Then watch it move: waiting, approved, paid.
